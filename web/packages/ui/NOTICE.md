Primitives generated by shadcn CLI 4.21.0, radix-nova registry, 2026-09-29.
47 primitive modules. Adaptations: local cn imports, shared semantic tokens,
English i18n labels, command-dialog accessible content placement and carousel
listener cleanup, slider thumb labels and accessible primary hover contrast.
